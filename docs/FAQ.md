# Kidcol FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

## English

### General Questions

#### What is Kidcol?
Kidcol (Kids Coloring Zone) is a digital coloring book application designed specifically for children. It allows young users to select coloring images from a remote server, color them digitally using various colors and brush sizes, organize their artwork into collections, and even print their creations as PDFs.

#### Who is Kidcol for?
Kidcol is designed for children of all ages who enjoy coloring and creative activities. The app is also suitable for parents looking for educational and creative digital activities for their children, as well as teachers who want to incorporate digital art into their classrooms.

#### What platforms does Kidcol support?
Kidcol is a cross-platform application built with Flutter, supporting:
- Android devices
- iOS devices (iPhone and iPad)
- Web browsers
- Windows computers
- macOS computers
- Linux computers

### Features and Usage

#### How do I start using Kidcol?
1. Open the app and navigate to the Home screen
2. Browse through the available coloring images
3. Select an image you want to color
4. The image will be ready for coloring with default tools
5. Save your artwork when finished

#### How do I create a collection?
1. From the Home screen, select "Create Collection"
2. Enter a name for your collection
3. Save the collection
4. Add your completed drawings to this collection for easy organization

#### How can I organize my drawings?
Kidcol allows you to organize your drawings into collections:
- Create multiple collections with different themes
- Add completed drawings to appropriate collections
- View all images in a collection
- Edit collection names or delete collections as needed

#### How do I print my colored images as PDFs?
1. Complete your coloring on the selected image
2. Save your artwork
3. Navigate to your collection containing the image
4. Select the image you want to print
5. Choose the "Print" or "Download as PDF" option
6. Follow the on-screen instructions to generate and save the PDF

### Account and Features

#### What features are available in Kidcol?
Kidcol offers the following features:
- Access to a variety of coloring images
- Ability to create and organize collections
- Basic coloring tools

#### Are there any limitations?
Currently, Kidcol provides:

### Data and Privacy

#### Where are my drawings stored?
Your drawings are stored in two ways:
- Locally on your device for immediate access
- In your Kidcol account (if you have one) for backup and cross-device access

#### Is my data secure?
Yes, Kidcol takes data security seriously:
- All data transmissions are encrypted
- Personal information is protected according to our Privacy Policy
- We comply with applicable data protection regulations
- We never share your personal information with third parties without consent

#### Does Kidcol collect personal information about children?
Kidcol is designed with children's privacy in mind:
- We collect minimal personal information necessary for the service
- We comply with children's privacy laws (such as COPPA)
- Parents can review, modify, or delete their children's information
- We don't use children's data for advertising purposes

### Troubleshooting

#### Why can't I download new coloring images?
This issue might be caused by:
- No internet connection
- Server maintenance
- Account limitations (for free accounts)
- App cache issues

Try these solutions:
- Check your internet connection
- Restart the app
- Clear the app cache
- Verify your account status

#### Why is the app running slowly?
Performance issues might be due to:
- Too many saved drawings
- Outdated app version
- Device memory limitations
- Background apps consuming resources

Try these solutions:
- Delete unused drawings or collections
- Update to the latest app version
- Close other apps running in the background
- Restart your device

#### Why can't I print my drawings?
Printing issues might be caused by:
- No printer connected
- Printer compatibility issues
- PDF generation errors
- Insufficient permissions

Try these solutions:
- Check your printer connection
- Ensure your printer is compatible
- Restart the app and try again
- Check app permissions

#### What should I do if I forgot my password?
1. Open the app and select "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your registered email or phone number
3. Follow the instructions to reset your password
4. Create a new password and log in

#### How do I contact customer support?
You can reach our customer support team through:
- The support option in the app settings
- Email: me@ajianaz.dev
- Website: www.ajianaz.dev

### Pemecahan Masalah

#### Mengapa saya tidak dapat mengunduh gambar mewarnai baru?
Masalah ini mungkin disebabkan oleh:
- Tidak ada koneksi internet
- Pemeliharaan server
- Batasan akun (untuk akun gratis)
- Masalah cache aplikasi

Coba solusi ini:
- Periksa koneksi internet Anda
- Mulai ulang aplikasi
- Hapus cache aplikasi
- Verifikasi status akun Anda

#### Mengapa aplikasi berjalan lambat?
Masalah kinerja mungkin disebabkan oleh:
- Terlalu banyak gambar yang disimpan
- Versi aplikasi yang kedaluwarsa
- Keterbatasan memori perangkat
- Aplikasi latar belakang yang mengkonsumsi sumber daya

Coba solusi ini:
- Hapus gambar atau koleksi yang tidak digunakan
- Perbarui ke versi aplikasi terbaru
- Tutup aplikasi lain yang berjalan di latar belakang
- Mulai ulang perangkat Anda

#### Mengapa saya tidak dapat mencetak gambar saya?
Masalah pencetakan mungkin disebabkan oleh:
- Tidak ada printer yang terhubung
- Masalah kompatibilitas printer
- Kesalahan pembuatan PDF
- Izin yang tidak mencukupi

Coba solusi ini:
- Periksa koneksi printer Anda
- Pastikan printer Anda kompatibel
- Mulai ulang aplikasi dan coba lagi
- Periksa izin aplikasi

2. Masukkan email atau nomor telepon yang terdaftar
3. Ikuti instruksi untuk mengatur ulang kata sandi Anda
4. Buat kata sandi baru dan masuk
