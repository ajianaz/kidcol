# Kidcol Privacy Policy

## English Version

### Introduction

Welcome to Kidcol (Kids Coloring Zone). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our digital coloring book application. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users, especially children who are our primary audience.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Kidcol mobile application. By using Kidcol, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

If you are a parent or guardian using this app on behalf of a child, this Privacy Policy also explains your rights and responsibilities regarding your child's information.

### Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

#### Device Information

When you use Kidcol, we automatically collect certain device information:

- Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Device brand and model
- Operating system and version
- Unique device identifier (device ID)
- Screen resolution and display information
- Device language settings

This information helps us optimize the app for different devices and troubleshoot technical issues.

#### Account Information

When you create an account with Kidcol, we collect:

- Collection count (number of artwork collections created)
- Email address (if provided)
- Account creation date and last activity timestamp

#### User-Generated Content

Kidcol allows users to create and store their own artwork. We collect and store:

- Colored images and drawings created by users
- Collection information (names, creation dates)
- Drawing data including color selections and brush strokes
- Metadata associated with your artwork

#### Network and Usage Data

We collect information about how you interact with our app:

- App usage frequency and duration
- Features used within the app
- Network connectivity information
- Error logs and crash reports
- Performance metrics

### How We Collect Information

We collect information through several methods:

- **Direct Collection**: When you provide information during account creation or verification
- **Automatic Collection**: Through the app's interaction with your device
- **Local Storage**: Using the Isar database on your device
- **Server Communication**: When the app communicates with our external servers (gateway.ajianaz.dev) to fetch coloring images

### How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

- **Service Provision**: To provide the coloring book functionality and maintain your account
- **Personalization**: To customize your experience and remember your preferences
- **Communication**: To send important account notifications and updates
- **Security**: To protect against unauthorized access
- **Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and improve our app's features and performance
- **Support**: To provide customer service and technical support
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

### Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously and implement appropriate measures to protect your information:

#### Local Storage

- Your artwork and app data are stored locally on your device using the Isar database
- This local storage ensures your drawings remain accessible even without an internet connection
- Local data is encrypted where technically feasible

#### Server Communication

- When communicating with our servers, we use industry-standard encryption protocols
- Coloring images are fetched from our external server (gateway.ajianaz.dev) using secure connections
- Account verification data is transmitted securely

#### Security Measures

- We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures
- Access to your data is limited to authorized personnel who need it to provide the service
- We regularly review our security practices to ensure they remain effective

### Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our app
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
- **Safety Protection**: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
- **Business Transfers**: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

### Children's Privacy

Kidcol is designed for children, and we take special care to protect their privacy:

- We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent
- We collect minimal information necessary to provide the coloring experience
- We do not use children's information for advertising or marketing purposes
- Parents can review, modify, or delete their children's information by contacting us


### Local Storage (Isar Database)

Kidcol uses the Isar database for local storage on your device:

- All your artwork and collections are stored locally using Isar
- This allows for fast access to your drawings without requiring constant internet connectivity
- Local storage ensures your creative work remains private to you
- The Isar database is optimized for mobile performance and efficiency
- You control this local data and can delete it through the app's interface

### Third-Party Services

Kidcol may integrate with third-party services to enhance functionality:

- **Image Servers**: We communicate with gateway.ajianaz.dev to fetch coloring images
- **Analytics Services**: We may use analytics tools to understand app usage patterns

These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

### Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access**: You can request access to the personal information we hold about you
- **Correction**: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Deletion**: You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations
- **Portability**: You can request a copy of your data in a portable format
- **Restriction**: You can request restriction of processing of your personal information
- **Objection**: You can object to certain types of processing of your personal information

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy.

### Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Kidcol may use cookies and similar tracking technologies:

- We use cookies to enhance your experience and remember your preferences
- We may use analytics cookies to understand how our app is used
- You can control cookie settings through your device preferences

### International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own:

- Our servers and service providers may be located in different jurisdictions
- When we transfer your information internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place
- We comply with applicable international data transfer regulations

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time:

- We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated policy in the app
- For material changes, we will provide at least 30 days' notice before they become effective
- Your continued use of the app after such notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

### Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

- Email: me@ajianaz.dev
- Website: www.ajianaz.dev

### Pendahuluan

Selamat datang di Kidcol (Kids Coloring Zone). Kebijakan Privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana kami mengumpulkan, menggunakan, menyimpan, dan melindungi informasi Anda saat menggunakan aplikasi buku mewarnai digital kami. Kami berkomitmen untuk melindungi privasi semua pengguna kami, terutama anak-anak yang merupakan audiens utama kami.

Kebijakan Privasi ini berlaku untuk semua pengguna aplikasi seluler Kidcol. Dengan menggunakan Kidcol, Anda menyetujui pengumpulan dan penggunaan informasi seperti dijelaskan dalam kebijakan ini.

Jika Anda adalah orang tua atau wali yang menggunakan aplikasi ini atas nama anak, Kebijakan Privasi ini juga menjelaskan hak dan tanggung jawab Anda mengenai informasi anak Anda.

### Informasi yang Kami Kumpulkan

Kami mengumpulkan beberapa jenis informasi untuk menyediakan dan meningkatkan layanan kami:

#### Informasi Perangkat

Saat Anda menggunakan Kidcol, kami secara otomatis mengumpulkan informasi perangkat tertentu:

- Jenis perangkat (mobil, tablet, desktop)
- Merek dan model perangkat
- Sistem operasi dan versi

- Resolusi layar dan informasi tampilan

Informasi ini membantu kami mengoptimalkan aplikasi untuk perangkat yang berbeda dan memecahkan masalah teknis.

#### Informasi Akun

Saat Anda membuat akun dengan Kidcol, kami mengumpulkan:

- Jumlah koleksi (jumlah koleksi karya seni yang dibuat)
- Alamat email (jika disediakan)
- Tanggal pembuatan akun dan timestamp aktivitas terakhir

#### Konten yang Dihasilkan Pengguna

Kidcol memungkinkan pengguna membuat dan menyimpan karya seni mereka sendiri. Kami mengumpulkan dan menyimpan:

- Gambar berwarna dan gambar yang dibuat oleh pengguna
- Informasi koleksi (nama, tanggal pembuatan)
- Data menggambar termasuk pemilihan warna dan sapuan kuas
- Metadata yang terkait dengan karya seni Anda

#### Data Jaringan dan Penggunaan

Kami mengumpulkan informasi tentang bagaimana Anda berinteraksi dengan aplikasi kami:

- Frekuensi dan durasi penggunaan aplikasi
- Fitur yang digunakan dalam aplikasi
- Informasi konektivitas jaringan
- Log kesalahan dan laporan crash
- Metrik kinerja

### Cara Kami Mengumpulkan Informasi

Kami mengumpulkan informasi melalui beberapa metode:

- **Pengumpulan Langsung**: Saat Anda memberikan informasi selama pembuatan akun atau verifikasi

- **Penyimpanan Lokal**: Menggunakan database Isar di perangkat Anda
- **Komunikasi Server**: Saat aplikasi berkomunikasi dengan server eksternal kami (gateway.ajianaz.dev) untuk mengambil gambar mewarnai

### Cara Kami Menggunakan Informasi Anda

Kami menggunakan informasi Anda untuk tujuan berikut:

- **Penyediaan Layanan**: Untuk menyediakan fungsionalitas buku mewarnai dan memelihara akun Anda
- **Personalisasi**: Untuk menyesuaikan pengalaman Anda dan mengingat preferensi Anda
- **Komunikasi**: Untuk mengirim notifikasi dan pembaruan akun penting
- **Keamanan**: Untuk melindungi dari akses tidak sah
- **Peningkatan**: Untuk menganalisis pola penggunaan dan meningkatkan fitur dan kinerja aplikasi kami
- **Dukungan**: Untuk menyediakan layanan pelanggan dan dukungan teknis
