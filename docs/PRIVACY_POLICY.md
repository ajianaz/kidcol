## __English Version__

## __Introduction__

Welcome to Kidcol (Kids Coloring Zone). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our digital coloring application.

Kidcol is designed for children, and we prioritize safety and privacy.
__Kidcol does not require an account, does not collect personal data, and does not ask for email or identity information.__

By using Kidcol, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## __Information We Collect__

Kidcol collects __minimal information__ and __never collects personal or identifiable data__.

We collect the following:

### __1. Device Information (Non-Personal)__

Automatically collected for app compatibility:

- Device type (phone/tablet)
- Operating system & version
- Screen resolution
- Language settings
- Device performance data

We do __not__ collect:

- Email
- Name
- Age
- Phone number
- Location
- Contact list
- Photos outside the app
- Advertising IDs

### __2. User-Generated Content__

Stored __only on your device__, never uploaded:

- Colored images
- Drawing data (colors, brush strokes)
- Artwork metadata
- Local collections

### __3. Local Usage Data__

Used only inside the device to improve your experience:

- Feature usage
- App session duration
- Local error logs

_(Not transmitted to any server)_

---

## __How Information Is Stored__

All data is stored in the user's device using the __Isar local database__.

- No cloud storage
- No remote server storage
- No backups created by us
- No background data collection

Deleting the app also deletes all stored data.

---

## __Server Communication (Images Only)__

Kidcol connects to our server __only to download coloring images__:

__gateway.ajianaz.dev__

✔ No user data is sent
✔ No tracking
✔ No analytics
✔ Secure HTTPS connection
✔ Images only, no personal info

---

## __How We Use Information__

Because Kidcol does not collect personal data, information is used only for:

- Displaying and saving artwork locally
- Improving app performance on your device
- Ensuring the app works correctly on various devices
- Loading coloring images from the server

We do __not__ use data for:

- Advertising
- Marketing
- Behavioral tracking
- Personal profiling

---

## __Children's Privacy__

Kidcol is built specifically for children:

- Fully compliant with COPPA and child-privacy principles
- No account creation
- No personal data collection
- No online interaction
- No ads based on behavior
- No tracking technologies

Parents can delete all data at any time directly from the device.

---

## __Data Sharing__

Because Kidcol does not collect personal data, we do not share any data with:

- Advertisers
- Analytics companies
- Third-party services

We only share data if required by law.

---

## __Your Rights__

Since all information is stored locally:

- You fully control your data on your device
- You may delete artwork anytime
- You may uninstall the app to remove all stored information

If you need assistance, you may contact us.
